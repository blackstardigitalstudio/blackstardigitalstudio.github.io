The 'War of the Stray Dog': How a Pet Sparked an International Crisis

In 1925, a border dispute between Greece and Bulgaria escalated into military conflict, allegedly triggered by a Greek soldier chasing his stray dog across the frontier into Bulgarian territory.

This bizarre event, known as the "Incident at Petrich," began when a Greek sentry was purportedly shot after crossing the border while retrieving his runaway dog. Greek forces subsequently invaded Bulgaria, capturing several villages and causing casualties. The League of Nations swiftly intervened, demanding a ceasefire and Greek withdrawal, ultimately imposing reparations on Greece. This swift international action is often cited as a success for early conflict resolution efforts, highlighting the League's nascent ability to prevent larger regional conflicts.
