Did you know? The American Mafia Helped the Allies Invade Sicily in WWII!

During World War II, notorious American Mafia boss Lucky Luciano secretly assisted U.S. Naval intelligence with Operation Husky, the Allied invasion of Sicily, leveraging his deep connections to the Sicilian underworld.

Luciano, then imprisoned in New York, was secretly approached by U.S. Navy intelligence officers who sought to exploit his powerful network. His contacts within the Sicilian Mafia provided crucial intelligence about local conditions, helped prevent sabotage of docks and ships in New York, and facilitated a smoother landing for Allied troops by minimizing resistance and aiding communication with local inhabitants. This controversial collaboration, sometimes referred to as 'Operation Underworld,' was a strategic alliance that ultimately contributed to Luciano's commutation and deportation to Italy after the war.
