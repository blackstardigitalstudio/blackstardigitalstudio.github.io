World's First Novel: A Thousand-Year-Old Japanese Epic of Love and Politics

Did you know the world's first true novel, *The Tale of Genji*, was written over a thousand years ago in Japan by a woman? Authored by Murasaki Shikibu in the early 11th century, this sophisticated work predates European novels by centuries.

Murasaki Shikibu, a lady-in-waiting at the Imperial court during the Heian period, penned this monumental work, a psychological study of Prince Genji and his descendants across generations. Comprising 54 chapters and over 1,000 pages, *Genji* explores themes of love, loss, politics, and the ephemeral nature of life with remarkable depth. Its narrative complexity, character development, and use of psychological realism set a benchmark for literary fiction that would not be paralleled in the West until much later, solidifying its place as a literary masterpiece.
