Munch's Secret Note on 'The Scream': A 'Madman's' Own Words?

Edvard Munch's iconic painting 'The Scream' harbors a faint, almost invisible inscription in its upper left corner: 'Can only have been painted by a madman!' For years, experts debated its origin, but it was confirmed to be Munch's own handwriting.

Advanced infrared analysis in 2021 unequivocally confirmed that the tiny pencil inscription was indeed written by Munch himself, dispelling long-standing theories of vandalism. Scholars now believe he added the phrase after a particularly harsh critical reception of his work at an exhibition in 1895, where a medical student suggested his mental state was evident in his art. This deeply personal, self-deprecating yet defiant note adds another layer of psychological depth to one of the world's most expressive masterpieces, reflecting the artist's tormented inner world and his response to public scrutiny.
