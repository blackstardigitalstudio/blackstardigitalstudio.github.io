The Immortal Jellyfish: Can it Really Cheat Death?

Did you know that a tiny jellyfish species, *Turritopsis dohrnii*, has earned the nickname "immortal jellyfish" because it can theoretically live forever by reversing its biological clock? When faced with stress or injury, it can transform back into an immature polyp state, effectively resetting its life cycle.

This unique ability, known as transdifferentiation, allows the adult jellyfish to revert its cells to an earlier developmental stage, bypassing death by old age or injury. While not truly immortal in the sense of being invulnerable, as they can still be eaten by predators or succumb to disease, their capacity to repeatedly cycle through life stages makes them a fascinating subject for longevity research. Scientists are studying *Turritopsis dohrnii* to understand the cellular mechanisms behind this remarkable biological feat, hoping to gain insights into regeneration and aging processes.
