Did you know? A Mysterious Hum Haunts Towns Around the World

In places like Taos, New Mexico, and Bristol, England, a small fraction of residents report hearing a persistent, low-frequency hum that significantly impacts their lives. Yet, its source remains a baffling scientific mystery, perceivable by only some individuals.

Dubbed 'The Hum,' this elusive phenomenon has been investigated with advanced audio equipment in locations globally since it first gained prominence in the early 1990s in Taos. Despite extensive research, no definitive environmental or industrial source has been identified for this constant drone. Theories range from distant industrial noise, natural seismic activity, or even an effect of tinnitus, to less conventional explanations. Scientists are still searching for a conclusive explanation for why only certain individuals can perceive this disruptive, unlocatable sound.
