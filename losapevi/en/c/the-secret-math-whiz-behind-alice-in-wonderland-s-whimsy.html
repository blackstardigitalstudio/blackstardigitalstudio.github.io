The Secret Math Whiz Behind Alice in Wonderland's Whimsy

''Alice's Adventures in Wonderland'' isn't just a whimsical tale; its author, Charles Lutwidge Dodgson (Lewis Carroll), was a logician and mathematician whose profession subtly infused the book with sophisticated mathematical humor and paradoxes.

Dodgson taught mathematics at Oxford and deliberately wove elements like symbolic logic, geometry, and number theory into Alice's journey. From the Mad Hatter's tea party, where time is an unsolvable riddle, to Alice's changing size representing transformations, the narrative playfully explores concepts that intrigued his academic peers. These hidden layers continue to fascinate scholars and mathematicians, demonstrating the depth behind the beloved children's classic.
